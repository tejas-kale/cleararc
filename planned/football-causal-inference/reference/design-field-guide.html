Choose a football causal design

If you have…Consider…Ask first…
Random allocationITT experiment analysisWhat unit was randomised? Is uncertainty cluster-aware?
Rich measured baseline causesMatching, weighting, standardisation or AIPWIs exchangeability plausible? Is there overlap?
Repeated outcomes and a comparison groupDiD/event studyWould untreated trends be parallel? Is timing staggered?
A deterministic thresholdSharp RDDAre potential outcomes continuous at the cut-off?
A threshold changing treatment probabilityFuzzy RDD/IVDo relevance, exclusion and monotonicity hold?
A policy that shifts treatmentIV/2SLSWhose LATE is identified? Could policy affect outcome directly?
One treated unit and donor historiesSynthetic controlIs pre-fit excellent? Are donors uncontaminated?
A long outcome series with one interruptionInterrupted time seriesWhat else changed at the same date?
Time-varying treatment and feedbackLongitudinal g-methodsDoes sequential exchangeability and positivity hold?
High-dimensional pre-treatment covariatesDML with cross-fittingIs the adjustment set causal, and are folds leakage-safe?
A mechanism questionCausal mediationWhat confounds mediator and outcome?
Time until injury or exitCausal survival analysisIs censoring informative? Which estimand is interpretable?

Universal six-line analysis brief

  1. Population and eligibility.
  2. Treatment strategies and comparator.
  3. Outcome and time horizon.
  4. Estimand and target population.
  5. Identification assumptions and causal graph.
  6. Estimator, diagnostics, uncertainty and sensitivity analyses.
Stop signal. If you cannot write lines 1–5, do not choose line 6 yet.
