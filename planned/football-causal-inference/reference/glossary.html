Football causal-inference glossary

ATE
Average of Y(1)−Y(0) in the target population.
ATT
Average effect among units that received treatment.
Consistency
Observed outcome under the received treatment equals the corresponding potential outcome; treatment versions are well defined.
Exchangeability
The comparison group represents the missing counterfactual, marginally or conditional on measured history.
Positivity
Every covariate profile in the target population has a non-zero probability of each strategy.
ITT
Effect of randomised assignment, regardless of adherence.
Confounder
A pre-treatment common cause of treatment and outcome in the assumed causal structure.
Mediator
A post-treatment variable on a pathway from treatment to outcome.
Collider
A common effect of two variables; conditioning on it can open a non-causal path.
Propensity score
Conditional probability of treatment given measured pre-treatment covariates.
Parallel trends
Without treatment, treated and comparison groups would have had the same average change.
LATE
Local average treatment effect among units whose treatment responds to the instrument under monotonicity.
RMST
Restricted mean survival time: expected event-free time up to a chosen horizon.
Target trial
The hypothetical randomised protocol an observational analysis aims to emulate.
Treatment-confounder feedback
A time-varying covariate is affected by past treatment and confounds later treatment.
Cross-fitting
Generating nuisance predictions for observations excluded from the corresponding model’s training fold.

Definitions follow the authorised local books, especially Hernán & Robins, Causal Inference: What If. Ask your teaching agent to add an example beside any definition you cannot retrieve unaided.
