# Football Causal Inference Resources

## Knowledge

- Local book: *Causal Inference for Data Science* — Aleix Ruiz de Villa Robert (2025).
  Use for: causal graphs, adjustment, instrumental variables, heterogeneous effects, double machine learning and time series.
- Local book: *Causal Inference: What If* — Miguel A. Hernán and James M. Robins (2020).
  Use for: counterfactuals, identification, target trials, g-methods, propensity methods, doubly robust estimators, mediation and survival analysis.
- Local book: *Mastering 'Metrics* — Joshua D. Angrist and Jörn-Steffen Pischke (2015).
  Use for: random assignment, regression, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity and differences-in-differences.
- Local book: *Experimentation for Engineers* — David Sweet (2023).
  Use for: experimental units, metrics, power, randomisation and practical experiment operations.
- Local book: *Econometrics* — Fumio Hayashi (2000).
  Use for: regression, instrumental variables, panel-data estimators and time-series foundations.
- Local book: *Introductory Econometrics: A Modern Approach* — Jeffrey M. Wooldridge (2016).
  Use for: omitted-variable bias, panel and fixed-effects models, instrumental variables, programme evaluation and time-series assumptions.
- [Football paper map](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jphys.2017.08.004).
  The local verified curriculum map supplies the complete paper-to-technique mapping; individual primary papers are linked from lessons and the course index.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Cross Validated — causal inference questions](https://stats.stackexchange.com/questions/tagged/causal-inference).
  Use for: testing a precisely stated identification or estimation question against expert critique.
- [The Society for Causal Inference](https://www.societyforcausalinference.org/).
  Use for: seminars and practitioner perspectives beyond textbook exercises.

## Gaps

- No strong standalone football paper was located for explicit DAG/back-door identification, longitudinal g-methods, target-trial emulation or standalone AIPW.
- The course therefore teaches these using clearly labelled constructed football scenarios grounded in the mapped studies.
