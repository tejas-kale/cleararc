# Teaching notes

- Use British English.
- Use DD.MM.YYYY for dates in prose.
- Keep each lesson focused on one practical decision.
- Football means association football/soccer.
- No prior causal-inference knowledge is assumed; algebra is introduced only when it clarifies an estimand or estimator.
- Do not create learning records until the learner demonstrates understanding.
- Write lessons in plain, literal prose with complete sentences. Avoid shorthand notes, slogan-like headings, em dashes, curly quotes and unexplained labels.
- A stated completion time must include enough time for the learner to read the explanation, work through the example and complete the exercises.
