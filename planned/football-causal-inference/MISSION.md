# Mission: Causal inference through football

## Why
Build the ability to turn football questions into defensible causal analyses: define the intervention and estimand, choose an identification strategy, expose assumptions, and communicate what the evidence can and cannot support.

## Success looks like
- Distinguish causal effects from predictive associations in football data.
- Design and critique experiments and quasi-experiments using football examples.
- Select an estimator only after stating the estimand and identification assumptions.
- Recognise common failures such as conditioning on mediators, regression to the mean and implausible parallel trends.

## Constraints
- Lessons are short, cumulative, interactive HTML documents written in British English.
- Causal concepts come only from the six authorised local books.
- Applications come from the verified football-paper map; constructed scenarios are labelled honestly.

## Out of scope
- A comprehensive mathematical treatment of asymptotic theory.
- Predictive football modelling without a causal question.
- Claims that a football paper exists for a mapped evidence gap.
