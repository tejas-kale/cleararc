The arc

Each lesson unlocks one job on the street. Do them in order; later weeks reuse the control tree from Lesson 1. Peak colour in Berlin is usually mid to late October — start the clock so Lessons 5–6 land in that window if you can.

#LessonWhat it unlocksTime
1 The camera you actually have One Fusion sensor, four honest framings, and why Pro folklore does not apply 8 min
2 Configure the Air once 24MP HEIF default, when 48MP is worth it, Grid, Level, Standard style 8 min
3 Protect the yellow Meter the leaf, AE/AF Lock, overcast as a softbox 8 min
4 Compose without an ultra-wide Foreground, height, one named subject — not a pavement document 8 min
5 Two scales without macro Place + belonging detail; closest focus; when to skip Portrait blur 8 min
6 Cull, two-minute edit, publish Same-day cull, Photos order of operations, Hugo JPEG 10 min

How to use this

Read one lesson, then do that week’s walk before opening the next. Answer quizzes before revealing. Bring “but surely the Air can…” questions back to your teacher — those are usually Pro folklore leaking in.
