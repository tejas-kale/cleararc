# iPhone Air autumn photography Resources

Sources used to write the lessons. Hardware claims come from Apple. Process claims that Apple does not document are labelled as project rules, not as specs.

## Knowledge

- [Apple: iPhone Air technical specifications](https://www.apple.com/iphone-air/specs/)
  Primary hardware source. Use for: Fusion Main 26 mm ƒ/1.6, optical-quality 2× at 52 mm, 24MP/48MP HEIF/JPEG only, panorama up to 63MP, Smart HDR 5, Night mode, Photographic Styles, Camera Control, missing ultra-wide/tele/macro/ProRAW.
- [Apple Newsroom: Introducing iPhone Air](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/09/introducing-iphone-air-a-powerful-new-iphone-with-a-breakthrough-design/)
  Launch framing of the single rear camera. Use for: confirming the Air is not a dual/triple system.
- [Apple Support: Use Photographic Styles](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iph629d2cd37/ios)
  How Styles sit in the capture pipeline, and that they can be changed later on HEIF. Use for: Standard vs Bright, Tone/Color/Intensity.
- [Apple Support: Set up your shot](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/set-up-your-shot-iph3dc593597/26/ios/26)
  Grid, Level, tap-to-focus, exposure sun, AE/AF Lock. Use for: Lessons 2–3 technique.
- [Apple Support: Edit photos and videos](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/edit-photos-and-videos-iphb08064d57/26/ios/26)
  What Photos can do without a third-party editor. Use for: Lesson 6 edit order.
- [AppleInsider: How to master the Camera app on iPhone Air](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/01/14/how-to-master-the-camera-app-on-iphone-air)
  Air-specific Camera UI (Resolution Control, Camera Control, customisable 28/35 mm). Use for: settings that Apple’s spec sheet does not walk through.
- [Trusted Reviews: Apple iPhone Air](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/apple-iphone-air)
  Independent confirmation of the single-camera layout. Use for: a non-Apple check that Pro folklore does not apply.
- [Issue #12](https://github.com/tejas-kale/blog/issues/12) and [research notes in PR #13](https://github.com/tejas-kale/blog/pull/13)
  The project constraints (neighbourhood, Photos-only, two-minute edit, Hugo export). Use for: mission and assignments, not for hardware facts.
- Berlin autumn timing: travel write-ups consistently put peak colour in **October**, especially mid-month, with usable colour into early November depending on frost ([Berlin Travel Tips](https://berlintraveltips.com/where-to-find-fall-foliage-in-berlin/), [GetYourGuide Berlin in fall](https://www.getyourguide.com/explorer/berlin-ttd17/berlin-in-fall/)). Use as a planning window, not a phenology paper. Destination parks are listed there; this course still prefers repeating nearby blocks.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Tejas has not asked to join a photography community for this project. Do not push Flickr groups or local camera clubs unless he asks.
- The real-world test is the blog itself: [tejas-kale.github.io/blog](https://tejas-kale.github.io/blog/). Weekly posts are the practice, not a forum critique.

## Gaps

- Apple does not publish a single table of which capture modes keep 48MP, Live Photos, Night mode, or depth data. Independent write-ups agree on the pattern used in Lesson 2; treat mode-by-mode exceptions as “typically”, not as a spec.
- No high-trust phenology series for *street-tree* colour in Berlin (as opposed to park foliage). Time the later weeks by looking at the control tree from Lesson 1, not by a calendar date.
- Minimum focus distance for the Air’s Fusion camera is not in Apple’s public spec sheet. Lesson 5 uses the hunt-then-step-back test instead of a millimetre figure.
