# iPhone Air autumn photography Glossary

Canonical language for this workspace. Terms below were introduced in the first arc and are **provisional** until Tejas uses them correctly on a walk or in a post.

The printable version is [reference/glossary.html](reference/glossary.html).

## The camera

**Fusion Main**:
The Air’s only rear camera: 48MP, 26 mm equivalent, ƒ/1.6, sensor-shift OIS. Every framing in this course is this sensor, cropped or not.
_Avoid_: the wide, the main lens as if a second lens existed

**Optical-quality 2×**:
A 12MP crop of the Fusion sensor at 52 mm equivalent, still with sensor-shift OIS. Not a second telephoto lens.
_Avoid_: optical zoom, telephoto

**24MP Fusion file**:
Apple’s default still: detail from the 48MP sensor fused with binned frames, with Smart HDR / Deep Fusion and, in Photo mode, depth data for later portraits.
_Avoid_: full resolution, RAW

**48MP HEIF**:
Optional high-resolution still in Photo mode. Larger, typically without the depth-for-later path, and unused by Night mode, flash, Live Photos, 2×, and Portrait.
_Avoid_: ProRAW — the Air cannot shoot ProRAW

## Capture

**AE/AF Lock**:
Touch-and-hold until the Camera app locks both exposure and focus, so a recompose does not re-meter the sky.
_Avoid_: tap to focus, when the lock is what you mean

**Photographic Style**:
A capture-time retuning of regions (not a global LUT). On HEIF it can be changed later in Photos. This course starts on Standard.
_Avoid_: filter, preset

**Bright**:
A Photographic Style Apple describes as brightening skin and adding a pop of vibrance. Usually too punchy for foliage.
_Avoid_: vivid, autumn look

## Editing and publishing

**Two-minute edit**:
The project cap in Photos: straighten, modest crop, highlights/shadows, optional warmth or Style intensity. Then stop.
_Avoid_: developing the file, grading

**Keeper**:
A frame that survives the same-day cull and is eligible for the blog JPEG.
_Avoid_: shot, capture, when you mean the one you will publish
