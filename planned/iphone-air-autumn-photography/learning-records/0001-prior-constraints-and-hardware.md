# Prior constraints and hardware (issue #12)

Tejas already knows the project shape: a month of short Berlin neighbourhood posts, shot on an iPhone Air, edited only in Photos, published to the blog. He also already knows the hardware limits in outline — one Fusion camera, no ultra-wide, no dedicated telephoto, no macro, no ProRAW — because they are the reason the series exists.

Future sessions should not re-litigate *whether* to use third-party apps or destination parks. The zone of proximal development is making those constraints produce publishable frames: exposure that keeps yellow texture, composition at 26 mm, an honest close-up, and a cull that a stranger can read as a place.

**Evidence:** [blog issue #12](https://github.com/tejas-kale/blog/issues/12) and the research/syllabus in [PR #13](https://github.com/tejas-kale/blog/pull/13). Depth claimed is planning knowledge, not demonstrated shooting skill — lessons are unread as of 6 September 2026.

**Implications:** Start with the camera map and settings, then light, then composition, then two scales, then publish. Do not open with “what is a photograph”.
