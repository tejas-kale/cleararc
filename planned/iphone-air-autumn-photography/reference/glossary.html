Glossary

Compressed language for the Air course. Provisional until these words show up, used correctly, in a walk or a post.

Fusion Main
The Air’s only rear camera: 48MP, 26 mm equivalent, ƒ/1.6, sensor-shift OIS. Every framing here is this sensor.Avoid: “the wide” as if a second lens existed
Optical-quality 2×
A 12MP crop of the Fusion sensor at 52 mm equivalent, still with sensor-shift OIS. Not a second telephoto lens.Avoid: optical zoom, telephoto
24MP Fusion file
Default still: 48MP detail fused with binned frames, Smart HDR / Deep Fusion, and (in Photo mode) depth data for later portraits.Avoid: full resolution, RAW
48MP HEIF
Optional high-resolution still in Photo mode. Typically drops the depth-for-later path. Night mode, flash, Live Photos, 2×, and Portrait do not give you this file.Avoid: ProRAW — the Air cannot shoot it
AE/AF Lock
Touch-and-hold until Camera locks exposure and focus, so a recompose does not re-meter the sky.Avoid: “tap to focus” when you mean the lock
Photographic Style
Capture-time retuning of regions, not a global LUT. Changeable later on HEIF. This course starts on Standard.Avoid: filter, preset
Bright
A Style Apple describes as brightening skin and adding a pop of vibrance. Usually too punchy for foliage.Avoid: vivid, autumn look
Two-minute edit
Project cap in Photos: straighten, modest crop, highlights/shadows, optional warmth or Style intensity. Then stop.Avoid: developing, grading
Keeper
A frame that survives the same-day cull and may become a blog JPEG.Avoid: “shot” when you mean the one you will publish
