Field checklist

Screenshot this. The walk is the lesson; this is only the pocket card.

  1. Grid and Level on; Photographic Style Standard; 24MP HEIF.
  2. 1× unless you have a reason for 2×. Digital zoom past 2× is scouting only.
  3. Tap the highlight you need to keep; drag exposure down. You can lift shadows later. You cannot restore a clipped yellow.
  4. AE/AF Lock when you recompose away from the leaf.
  5. Clean the lens (jacket pockets). Elbows in. Camera Control instead of pinch-zoom.
  6. If autofocus hunts, you are too close: step back, then 2× if you still need tightness.
  7. After rain, look down (puddles) and up (clearing sky through canopy).
  8. Cull the same day. Two-minute edit, then stop.
