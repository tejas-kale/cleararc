## Teaching notes
- The paper is the spine. Each lesson unlocks one of the five requested headings.
- Hardest idea: **why metro targeting is endogenous**, then why two *different* designs (shift-share vs within-metro distance) are not the same robustness check restated.
- Second-hardest: **media as treatment**, not as noise. Perceptions and presence, not ICE’s internal yield.
- National $3–14bn is an **extrapolation**. Keep the per-POI-week IV estimate and the scale-up in separate mental bins.
- IHS is “inverse hyperbolic sine”; the draft sometimes writes “sign”. Teach the correct name.
