Reference · ICE-ing the Economy

Glossary

Canonical vocabulary for reading Hernandez (2026). Aliases to avoid are the words that collapse distinct objects.

Prints to one or two pages

POI
A geocoded commercial establishment in Advan or SafeGraph.Avoid: “store” when workplaces are in the sample
CBSA / metro
The treatment unit. Eleven large areas use Metropolitan Divisions.Avoid: county, unless you are talking about someone else’s paper
Enforcement event
A news-extracted, geocodable interior operation or presence. 5,388 after dedup and audit.Avoid: ICE arrest count — that is yield, not presence
Onset (absorbing)
First post-20 Jan 2025 week with 3+ events in 14 days. 100 of 935 metros. Stays on.Avoid: “the week of the raid at this POI”
Shift-share instrument
2010 ACS foreign-born share × national weekly event count. Instruments onset in 2SLS with POI and state×week FE, cluster CBSA.
Spatial intensity
Running-minimum miles to the nearest raid to date, binned, estimated in treated metros with POI and CBSA×week FE. No instrument.
IHS
Inverse hyperbolic sine of visits or spend. Approximate percent interpretation; handles zeros. The draft sometimes writes “sign.”
Chilling
Withdrawal of activity by people who are not themselves detained, via perceived risk.Avoid: using it as a synonym for the IV coefficient
Focused vs general
Prior regimes weighted toward people with criminal records; 2025 interior operations are described as broader and more visible (East, Cox and Patler).
Design A / Design B
This course’s names for metro-onset IV versus within-metro distance. The paper does not number them this way.
