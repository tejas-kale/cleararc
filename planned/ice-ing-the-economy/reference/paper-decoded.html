Reference · ICE-ing the Economy

The paper, decoded

Hernandez (2026) walked claim by claim. Short quotations for study; the draft remains the author’s.

Spine: ICE-ing the Economy, 13 May 2026

“In January 2025, U.S. immigration enforcement expanded sharply and moved from the border to the interior.”

Lesson 1. Qualitative change, not only more of 2017–2020. East, Cox and Patler document who is arrested; this paper documents local activity.

“I estimate the economic spillovers … using data on 5,388 geocoded ICE raids combined with foot-traffic and spending data for 5.4 million points of interest.”

Lesson 2. Raids are news-NLP, not FOIA. Spend is ~1.1 million matched POIs, not 5.4 million.

“Foot traffic falls 2.7% and spending declines 6.2% per POI per week—or 8.1 billion fewer visits and $3–14 billion in foregone spending in a single year.”

Lesson 4. First clause is Design A IV. Second clause is a scale-up: $3.1bn on SafeGraph coverage, $14bn if those averages are painted onto all visit POIs.

“To rule out differences across targeted and non-targeted locations … I employ a shift-share instrument. I also assess the spatial decay of the effect within targeted metros as an alternative empirical strategy.”

Lesson 3. These are not two prints of one robustness table. Different samples, different FE, different estimands.

“The chilling is demographically indiscriminate … Economic activity decreases both on the supply side (workers not showing up) and on the demand side … Shoppers do not substitute to online channels.”

Lesson 4. “Indiscriminate” means both sides of the metro median are negative, not that the point estimates are equal. Online = merchant digital channel; delivery-app overlap falls.

“The breadth of the local economic damage mirrors the breadth of the enforcement approach.”

This is the thesis sentence. It is only as strong as the generality tables (Fig. 2–3) plus Design B’s distance gradient.

“A legitimate national debate over the appropriate scope of enforcement should weigh these spillovers explicitly, particularly because they concentrate in local economies that disproportionately depend on small, independent businesses.”

Policy paragraph. The empirical hook is the independent-vs-chain interaction, not a municipal finance model (which is not estimated).

“Future work should … explore longer-term outcomes of sustained enforcement such as entrepreneurship, investment, and innovation.”

Lesson 5. Explicitly not in this draft. First-year visits and spend only.
