Rules that always hold for this paper

  1. Treatment is a metro burst, not a body count. Three events in 14 days after inauguration, then absorbing.
  2. Media measures presence and perception. Official arrests measure yield and geocode poorly.
  3. Two designs, two estimands. IV: targeted vs not. Distance: closer vs farther inside targeted metros.
  4. Keep percents and billions in separate bins. −2.73% / −6.18% are IV. $3–14bn is a scale-up with a coverage fork.
  5. Generality is the claim. Below-median immigrant cells still fall; workplaces fall more than shops; independents more than chains.
  6. Null online is not “consumption vanished.” It is the merchant’s own digital channel plus falling delivery-app overlap.
