# ICE-ing the Economy — a teaching workspace

A short course that takes a data scientist through Hernandez (2026), *ICE-ing the Economy*: why the paper exists, what was measured, how identification is claimed, what the estimates say, and what remains open.

Five lessons, about forty minutes of reading. Built with the `teach` skill workflow: the working paper is the spine.

## Start here

Open **[`index.html`](index.html)** in a browser.
