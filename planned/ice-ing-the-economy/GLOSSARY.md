# Glossary

Canonical language for this workspace. Printable version: [reference/glossary.html](reference/glossary.html).

**POI (point of interest)**
A geocoded establishment (store, workplace, venue) in Advan/SafeGraph.

**CBSA / metro**
Core-Based Statistical Area. Treatment is defined at this (or Metropolitan Division) level, not at the POI.

**Raid / enforcement event**
A news-extracted, geocodable instance of interior immigration enforcement presence — not an official arrest count.

**Treatment onset (absorbing)**
First week after 20 Jan 2025 in which a metro has 3+ events in a 14-day window. Once on, it stays on in the sample.

**Shift-share (Bartik) instrument**
2010 foreign-born share times national weekly raid count.

**Spatial intensity / epicenter**
Within treated metros, distance from a POI to the nearest raid to date, binned (0–0.5 mi, …, 10+ mi).

**IHS (inverse hyperbolic sine)**
Transformation of visits and spend used in estimation so coefficients read as approximate percent changes, including zeros.

**Chilling effect**
Behavioural withdrawal (fewer visits, less spend, absenteeism) driven by perceived enforcement risk, not only by removal of arrested people.

**Supply-side vs demand-side (in this paper)**
Workplace POI visits (workers not showing up) vs consumer-facing visits and card spend (shoppers not coming or spending less).
