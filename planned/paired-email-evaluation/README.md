# Paired email evaluation — a teaching workspace

Five short, interactive lessons on sizing and interpreting a personal evaluation of three email-classification models on the same hand-labelled emails.

Open **[`index.html`](index.html)** in a browser.

| # | Lesson | Main question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [80 out of 100 is an estimate](lessons/0001-an-estimate-not-a-verdict.html) | What does observed accuracy tell you about a model? |
| 2 | [Why 200 changes the picture](lessons/0002-precision-and-rare-classes.html) | How does sample size affect common and rare outcomes? |
| 3 | [Compare models email by email](lessons/0003-paired-comparisons.html) | What evidence determines a paired difference? |
| 4 | [Count email units, not decisions](lessons/0004-dependence-and-calibration.html) | Why are dependence and calibration the constraints? |
| 5 | [Choose evidence for the claim](lessons/0005-a-defensible-case-study.html) | What can 100, 200, and 300 emails support? |

The course is planned and unread. The source analysis is the personal evaluation sample-size note and notebook, dated 21 September 2026.
