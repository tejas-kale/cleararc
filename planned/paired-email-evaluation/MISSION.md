# Mission: Reason about a paired email evaluation

## Why

A personal email evaluation needs evidence sized to its claims. The learner should be able to explain why 100 labelled emails can reveal obvious patterns, why it cannot reliably settle close model rankings or rare-class claims, and why evaluating every model on the same emails changes the analysis.

## Success looks like

- Can calculate and interpret a Wilson interval for 80 correct predictions in 100 emails.
- Can distinguish uncertainty for one accuracy, a paired difference, a rare class, probability quality, and calibration.
- Can explain why only model disagreements contribute to a paired accuracy difference.
- Can defend 200 emails as a stronger default while keeping 100 useful as an exploratory personal case study.

## Constraints

- This is a descriptive case study of one inbox, not a population-wide benchmark.
- Every selected email is scored by every model.
- Planning figures come from `email-evaluation-sample-size.md` and its companion notebook.
- Do not turn the course into an infrastructure or blog-planning exercise.
