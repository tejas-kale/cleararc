Teaching workspace

Self-improving AI agents

Seventeen short lessons distilling Stanford's CS329A into the parts a practitioner can act on: what to tune, what to measure, what to ignore, and one question worth answering yourself.

About 2 hours 40 minutes end to end · Built 21 August 2026 from the nine 2026 lectures · Arc V added 23 August 2026

This is not a summary of the course. It is the course re-cut for someone who uses agents rather than researches them, so every lesson ends with a dial to turn or a number to collect. Every lesson also carries a project hook, which is a testable question that lesson raises and the measurement that would answer it, and Arc V turns one of those into a real experiment. Read MISSION.md for why it is shaped this way.

These lessons are one half of the loop. The other half is asking your teacher about the runs that confuse you. Bring a failing trace, a benchmark number you do not trust, or a paper you cannot place on the map. If you are starting Arc V, bring the question before you build anything, because scoping is where a second opinion is worth the most.
