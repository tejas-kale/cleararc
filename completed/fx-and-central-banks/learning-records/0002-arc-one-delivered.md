# First arc delivered: exchange rates → reserves → consolidation → carry

Lessons 0001–0004 were written and delivered in a single session on 2026-08-20, built backwards from *The Economist*'s "When Japan buys yen, it unwinds a dangerous trade" so that every concept earns its place by unlocking a specific sentence in that article.

The arc's load-bearing idea is **consolidation** (Lesson 3): netting the treasury and the central bank into one entity, which reveals that quantitative easing swapped long fixed-rate debt for overnight floating-rate debt rather than reducing it. Everything in Lesson 4 — why Japan is a carry trader, why rate rises hurt quickly, why sterilisation does not cancel the intervention — depends on it.

**Why it matters:** this is *coverage, not yet learning*. No evidence of retention has been gathered. The quizzes and retrieval prompts are embedded in the lessons but their results are not visible to the teacher.
