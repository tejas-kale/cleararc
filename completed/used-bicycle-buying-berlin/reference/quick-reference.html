Used bicycle at the Berlin Flohmarkt

Best fit for this mission: a trekking bike or upright hybrid with 28-inch wheels, moderately wide grippy tyres, reliable brakes, low gears, mudguards, lights, a strong stand and a child-seat-compatible rack or seat-tube mounting point.

Look for

  • Sound frame and fork; no cracks, buckles or suspicious repainting.
  • Wheels run true; no loose spokes, hub play or tyre bulges.
  • Firm brakes and every gear shifts under load.
  • Comfortable reach and slightly bent knee at the bottom of the pedal stroke.
  • Exact child-seat manual checked against the bicycle.

Walk away

  • Ground-off or altered frame number.
  • Seller refuses ID, ownership explanation or written receipt.
  • Crack, deep dent, severe corrosion or impact damage.
  • Brake lever pulls to the bar, wheel wobbles or headset knocks.
  • Rack is not explicitly approved for a child seat.

Five-question purchase gate

  1. Is the frame/fork sound and the frame number intact?
  2. Does it fit and handle safely?
  3. Do brakes, gears, wheels and lights work—or are repairs costed?
  4. Can the exact child-seat system be mounted within all ratings?
  5. Has the seller accepted a written contract and proved ownership?
